Compact the given thread/context into a short summary. One job: reduce, don't add.

1. Extract the goal, what was decided, what remains open, and the next action.
2. Keep it short — a few bullet lines or a short paragraph. Cut narrative; keep decisions and pointers to artifacts (`.agents/artifacts/<task-slug>/`).
3. Do not research, design, or implement. Do not change files.
